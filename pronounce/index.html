Spelling Garden · pronunciation check

These are the words a child practises in the Spelling Garden app, read by computer voices. Please listen to each word. The first version is the one the app uses now; if it sounds right, just move on. If not, play the others and tick the one that sounds right, or tick “None is right”. You can add a short note to any word. Your ticks are saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back. When you’re done, press Send my choices.
