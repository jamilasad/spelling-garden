# 13 · Buzzy peeking over the keyboard

For Bee Mode's "Type it" keyboard: Buzzy peeks over the top edge of the honey-coloured key panel, next to the box that shows her typed word. He pops up for each word and hops when she presses a key.

The straight cut at the bottom of the picture sits on the key panel's top edge, so he looks like he's behind it with his hands on the edge.
